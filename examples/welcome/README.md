# Example: Welcome to Clipcourse

A three-chapter course that shows the main player features:

1. **Welcome** – text, boxes and shapes appearing over time; waits for _Continue_.
2. **The timeline is the beat** – clips switching at exact times; continues automatically.
3. **Video, audio and captions** – a video and an audio clip with WebVTT captions.

A logo in the course layer stays visible in all chapters.

All files in this directory were created for Clipcourse (the video and audio
were generated with FFmpeg test sources) and are released under CC0 1.0.
